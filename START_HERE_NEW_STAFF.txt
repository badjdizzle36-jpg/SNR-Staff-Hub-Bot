SNR BUNS — AUTOMATIC NEW STAFF ACCESS
21 September 2026

This update includes the previous City Run/Discord improvements as well as
staff onboarding. It is an update for your existing repository, not a complete
standalone installation.

BEFORE DEPLOYING
1. Open https://discord.com/developers/applications
2. Select your SNR bot application → Bot.
3. Under Privileged Gateway Intents, turn ON Server Members Intent and save.
   This must be enabled before starting the updated bot. Otherwise Discord can
   reject the bot's connection. Message Content Intent is not required here.
4. In your Discord server, give the BOT Manage Roles and Manage Channels.
   Ensure it can manage channel permissions across all categories/channels.
   Move the bot's role ABOVE the New Staff role. Do not give New Staff admin.

INSTALL
5. Extract the ZIP and copy all supplied files/folders beside your existing
   bot.py, replacing matching files. Include staff_onboarding.py,
   city_dashboard.py and city_notifications.py.
6. Keep all other repository files, your database, Railway volume and existing
   environment variables. Never replace the repository or database wholesale.
7. Commit and push with GitHub Desktop, then wait for Railway to deploy.
8. As an SNR Owner, run this command in your Discord:
   /snrhub_new_staff_setup
   staff_category: choose your exact SNR staff category
   general_chat: choose the exact general chat you want staff to use
   new_staff_role: choose your existing New Staff role
   If you leave new_staff_role blank, the bot reuses one uniquely named
   New Staff role or creates it with no server-wide permissions.
9. Put the permanent SNR staff panel in a permitted staff channel. An owner
   can run /snrhub_panel there if needed. Existing panel buttons still work.
10. Check with an ordinary test member that New Sale works, general chat can
    be read/written, outside channels are hidden and owner controls refuse
    access. Do not test restrictions while using an administrator account.

BEHAVIOUR
- New human members joining this configured server receive New Staff.
  This assumes your Discord invitations are for staff. It does not know who
  has been hired: every new human join is eligible, not just selected invites.
- Bots are skipped. Existing named SNR Staff/Management/Owner members retain
  their existing access. Members from before setup are not bulk-assigned.
- Joins missed while the bot is offline are recovered after reconnect.
- Configuration is stored in your existing persistent database.
- New Staff can use normal staff controls, including New Sale, customer tools,
  shifts and operational queues. Existing owner-only controls stay protected.
  A shared panel may still show an Owner/Admin button, but New Staff alone
  cannot pass the owner permission check.
- Allowed channels: the selected staff category and its ordinary channels,
  plus the selected general chat (even if general is outside that category).
- General chat gets view, history and send permission. Staff channels also
  permit application commands and normal voice participation where relevant.
- Explicit staff-denied or explicitly management-only channels stay hidden.
  If a legitimate staff channel is denied to the existing SNR Staff role,
  review that restriction first instead of granting New Staff an admin role.
- Other categories/channels receive a New Staff View Channel denial.
- Channel creation, moves and overwrite changes are handled while the bot is
  online. Rerun setup after channel changes made while the bot was offline.
  Gateway updates are asynchronous: create sensitive channels with private
  permissions from the start, rather than relying on a later bot update.
- Setup checks bot permissions/hierarchy and rejects privileged New Staff
  roles. If setup fails partway, it attempts to restore changed overwrites.
  If rollback also fails, automatic assignment is disabled and setup reports
  that a manual permissions review is needed.

DISCORD PERMISSION LIMIT
Discord combines all a member's roles and member-specific overwrites.
New Staff alone is restricted as above. Another role's explicit channel allow
or a member-specific allow can grant extra visibility; Administrator bypasses
channel restrictions entirely. Do not let another bot assign a broader role
to new joiners. This update preserves other roles and their overwrites rather
than deleting or stripping existing users' access.
Official reference: https://docs.discord.com/developers/topics/permissions
Intent reference: https://discordpy.readthedocs.io/en/stable/intents.html
