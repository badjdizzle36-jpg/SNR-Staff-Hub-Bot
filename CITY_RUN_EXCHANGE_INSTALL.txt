SNR CITY RUN — STICKER EXCHANGE UPDATE

INSTALL
1. Download and extract SNR_City_Run_Sticker_Exchange_Update.zip.
2. Open your local SNR-Staff-Hub-Bot repository in File Explorer.
3. Copy all supplied .py files and city-run-board-v4.png into the folder
   containing your existing bot.py. Choose Replace for matching filenames.
4. Leave your existing artwork folders, other assets, database, and environment
   settings in place. This update contains no customer database or credentials.
5. In GitHub Desktop, commit with summary: Add City Run Sticker Exchange.
6. Push origin. Wait for Railway's deployment to succeed.
7. Log in, open City Run, then tap STICKER EXCHANGE.

No manual database migration is needed. The exchange table is created on startup.
Keep your normal database backup before deploying an update.

PLAYER EXPERIENCE
- Open City Run > Sticker Exchange.
- Make a swap offer: choose a spare business, then a different business of the
  same rarity. The two actual sticker illustrations appear before publication.
- Publication is the maker's agreement to exchange exactly those two stickers.
- Other logged-in players see the offer and its expiry time.
- A player with an available duplicate of the requested business can review
  the exact exchange, then press Confirm this swap.
- Both transfers happen together. Both boards and collections update.
- My offers & swap history shows accepted, cancelled, open and expired offers.
- Refresh offers to load changes made by other players.

RULES THAT PROTECT YOUR ECONOMY
- One duplicate for one duplicate, same rarity only.
- Each player always retains their first copy of every business.
- No RP money, points, purchases or newly minted stickers are involved.
- Listed duplicates are reserved. Other open offers cannot spend them.
- Maximum five open offers per player; offers expire after 24 hours.
- The maker can cancel their own offer, including while a season is paused.
- Only an active season permits creating or accepting a swap.
- No cross-season swaps. Changed rarity rules are checked again on acceptance.
- A business with no other business of the same rarity cannot be listed.
  For example, if SNR Buns is the only ultra-rare business, it remains excluded
  from swaps under this rule. Its rarity is not changed by this update.
- Retry clicks do not transfer twice. Competing accepts have only one winner.

REWARDS AND DISCORD
- Completed swaps, completed sets and newly unlocked corners use the existing
  durable City Run event queue and private City Run Reward Claims channel.
- The same bot event delivery loop retries queued notices after failures.
- Ensure that private channel is configured and the bot can view it/send embeds.
- Route and grand-prize eligibility are recalculated from the actual collection.
- Corner bonuses are issued once, in the swap transaction.
- Players still request unlocked rewards; staff retain reward handover control.
- Open offers are displayed in the app, not broadcast into Discord.
- Completed swaps have a permanent database record and audit entry.
