SNR BUNS — LIVE MARKETPLACE & COMPACT PHONE UPDATE
22 September 2026 — read this file first

INSTALL
1. Extract the ZIP and copy all supplied files/folders into your EXISTING
   repository beside bot.py, replacing matching files.
2. Include the NEW compact_market.py and city-run-thumbs folder.
   The existing city_dashboard.py, city_trades.py and web_portal.py are updated.
3. Keep your database, .env, Railway volume and all other repository files.
   This is a cumulative update package, not a standalone replacement project.
4. Commit and push to GitHub, then wait for Railway deployment to finish.
5. Fully close/reopen the website or the phone app to load the updated page.
   No Discord setup command needs to be rerun for this marketplace update.
6. Read the in-game check below. The existing website URL and QR stay the same.

LIVE MARKETPLACE
- Opens by default and shows OTHER players' open offers clearly.
- Each compact listing names the seller, the sticker you receive, the sticker
  you give, rarity and expiry. Eligible players get Confirm swap.
- Three offers per page, previous/next links and rarity filters.
- Refreshes every 15 seconds while the page is visible, with a manual Refresh
  link and an honest refresh/login error message if the request fails.
- My duplicates contains your available spare stickers only.
- Offer this card opens Make offer with that specific sticker selected.
- Make offer uses ordinary selectable rows. No native dropdown popup is needed.
- Publish returns directly to Live Marketplace with confirmation. Your own
  offer is in My activity and appears in everyone else's public feed.
- My activity shows your offers/trades with pagination and cancel controls.
- Expired, accepted and cancelled offers do not remain in the live listings.
- Matching rarity, first-copy protection, reservation limits, account checks,
  expiry, stock and atomic transfer checks remain enforced by the server.

LB PHONE — WHAT IS VERIFIED / WHAT NEEDS AN IN-GAME CHECK
The web controls were tested with touch and inside an embedded Chromium
window. This chat has no connection to your running FiveM/LB Phone resource,
so this is not a claim of a live in-game test or guaranteed compatibility with
an unknown LB version/configuration. Login credentials/cookie protection and
CSRF checks were not weakened.

After deploying, open the site in your existing LB Phone app and:
1. Log in, open City Run, and expand/collapse a detail row.
2. Open Live Marketplace, tap rarity filters and Next/Previous.
3. Offer a spare, choose the desired business, and publish.
4. Have another logged-in player check the offer on their phone.
5. Cancel the test offer in My activity (or agree on a real swap together).

If the entire app cannot log in, load, or receive input in game while it works
in a normal browser, send the exact symptom and a screenshot/video. The server
owner may need to check the LB app's embedding/focus configuration; that cannot
be changed by uploading this website ZIP to Railway.
Official LB custom-app reference: https://docs.lbscripts.com/phone/custom-apps/
